# Where the game's art and sound come from

Kept up to date so the Google Play listing and an in-game credits screen can be written from it.

| What | Source | Terms, as Pon understands them (check again before release) |
|---|---|---|
| Card paintings (batches 1–3), Durgs, battlefields, title painting | Generated by Pon with ChatGPT image generation, from Claude's prompt sheets | OpenAI's terms assign the output to the user. Purely AI-made images may not be protected by copyright, so others could copy them. |
| Sound effects (21 files in `art/source/sfx/`) | Pixabay | Pixabay Content License: free for commercial use, no credit required. Not to be redistributed on their own (inside the game is fine). Credit is appreciated. |
| Music (6 pieces, `music_*.mp3`) | Generated by Pon with Google Gemini (Lyria) | Pon confirmed (5 Nov 2026) that Gemini-generated music may be used in a commercial game. Keep a copy of the terms page as it read then. |
| Fonts | Cormorant Garamond, Tiro Devanagari Hindi (bundled via @fontsource) | SIL Open Font License: free to use and bundle. |

Pixabay sounds: keep a note of each file's Pixabay page (title and author) as they are added, for an optional credits screen.

Regalia medallions (24, `rg_<empire>_<n>`, Maurya, Gupta, Chalukya, Chola): painted with ChatGPT image generation from Claude's prompts. Several shapes are informed reconstructions, not surviving objects (the Maurya drum and sword, the Gupta horse standard, the Chalukya shield, goad and boar standard, the Chola anklets, lamp and crown); treat them as illustrative.
