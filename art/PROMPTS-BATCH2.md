## Same rules as batch 1

- Paste the **era style block** for that card's empire first, then the card prompt. Use the same image tool you used for batch 1.
- Cards: portrait 5:7, as large as the tool allows. Battlefields and the title: landscape 16:9.
- Add: *"full figure with clear space above the head and below the feet"* for people.
- No text, borders or watermarks.
- Name each file exactly as listed and upload it to the same GitHub folders as before: `art/source/cards/` for cards, `art/source/battlefields/` for battlefields and the title.

## Era style blocks

The empires lived a thousand years apart, so clothing and weapons change. Pick the block for the card's empire.

**Maurya and neutral cards** (same as batch 1)

```
Indian miniature painting in the style of the Mughal-Rajput court ateliers, reimagined as rich game card art. Mineral pigments: lapis blue, vermilion, malachite green, yellow ochre, touches of real gold leaf. Fine brushwork, flat decorative perspective with gentle depth and soft modelling of faces and muscles. Warm oil-lamp light from the upper left. Clothing, weapons and architecture of ancient India, around 320 BCE, as seen in the stone reliefs of Sanchi and Bharhut: cotton dhoti and uttariya, cloth turbans, heavy gold and bead jewellery, no stirrups, no armour plates, no metal helmets. Single clear subject, centred, full of character, readable at small size. Dark warm background that fades toward the edges. No text, no letters, no frame, no border, no watermark.
```

**Gupta** (4th–5th century CE): replace the "Clothing, weapons…" sentence with:

```
Clothing, jewellery and architecture of the Gupta golden age, around 380 CE, as seen in the Ajanta murals and Gupta gold coins: fine translucent muslin, curled hair with jewelled bands, ornate gold crowns for royalty, long bows, straight swords, early Hindu temples with carved doorways, no stirrups, no plate armour.
```

**Chalukya** (7th century CE, the Deccan):

```
Clothing, weapons and architecture of the Chalukya Deccan around 620 CE, as seen in the rock-cut temples of Badami and Aihole: red sandstone cliffs, cave temples, warriors in short dhotis with broad belts, round shields, curved swords, tall tiered crowns for royalty, no plate armour.
```

**Chola** (11th century CE, Tamil coast):

```
Clothing, weapons and architecture of the Chola empire around 1014 CE, as seen in Chola bronzes and the Brihadeeswarar temple at Thanjavur: bare-chested warriors with gold armlets and waist sashes, long spears and swords, tall temple towers (vimana), the sea and wooden ships, bronze-coloured light.
```

**Rajput** (10th–12th century CE, north-west India):

```
Clothing, weapons and architecture of the early Rajput kingdoms around 1150 CE: quilted cotton armour coats and mail, round shields with bosses, curved talwar swords, lances, stirrups and armoured horses, hill forts of sandstone, saffron and silver colours.
```
