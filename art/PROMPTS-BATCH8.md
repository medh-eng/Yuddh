# YUDDH art prompts: batch 8, "The March South" (Mahakantara and Kanchi)

Two battles of the Gupta campaign, after **The Digvijaya** (batch 6). Around 340 CE Samudragupta, having broken the Naga kings, turned south down the **Dakshinapatha**, the southern road. His court poet and minister **Harishena** carved the record on the Allahabad (Prayaga) Pillar, and it names twelve southern kings he defeated. Two of them are our battles:

- **Vyaghraraja of Mahakantara.** *Vyaghra* means tiger, *Mahakantara* means the Great Forest. Where that forest was is argued (the forest country of Odisha and central India is the usual guess), so the game paints it as a country, not a map point.
- **Vishnugopa of Kanchi.** Kanchi (Kanchipuram) was the Pallava capital, far down the east coast, the furthest point the inscription reaches. Kanchi was already famous as a city of learning.

The inscription also records Samudragupta's policy: he captured these kings and then **released them and gave their kingdoms back** as vassals. The Gupta card of this batch is built on that.

The dates and the fights themselves are not known; the Pillar is the victor's own poem. The game invents the battles and says so in its History panel. Mahakantara is a battle of **ambush**: the forest hides things and the defenders set traps. Kanchi is a battle of **walls and learning**: a rich walled city with elephants and chariots.

This batch is **17 images**: 2 battlefields, 2 Durgs, 5 Mahakantara cards, 5 Kanchi cards, 3 Gupta cards. Paint the two battlefields and the two Durgs first (they change the look of both battles), then each side's cards, then the Gupta cards.

## Same rules as before

- The **Gupta block** is the Maurya-and-neutral era style block from `PROMPTS-BATCH2.md` with its clothing sentence swapped for the Gupta one (exactly as in batch 6). Gupta cards and the two battlefields use it as it is; the two new peoples use it with the clothing sentence swapped again, as below.
- Add "full figure with clear space above the head and below the feet" for single-person cards.
- Upload cards and Durgs to `art/source/cards/`, battlefields to `art/source/battlefields/`, named exactly as below.
