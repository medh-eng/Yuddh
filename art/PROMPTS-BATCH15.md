# YUDDH art prompts: batch 15, "Pullalur" (Chalukya campaign, battle 2, c. 618–630 CE)

After the Narmada, Pulakeshin II turned south-east. The Aihole inscription (Ravikirti, 634–635 CE) says he **defeated the Pallava king Mahendravarman I at Pullalur**, a place near **Kanchipuram** (the exact spot is argued over), and that the Pallava then **hid behind the walls of Kanchi** while Pulakeshin reached the **Kaveri** and the Chola, Pandya and Kerala kings came to terms. This is again the Chalukya court's own praise-poem; the Pallava side survives in Mahendravarman's own inscriptions (he was a king-poet, playwright and builder of rock-cut temples, "Vichitrachitta", curious-minded) and in the later Pallava account of revenge: his son **Narasimhavarman I** took the Pallava war to Badami in 642 CE. The game plays the **first round**: you are Pulakeshin II.

**Why this battle:** the Narmada was a river you held; Pullalur is a **field fight against a fortified, clever enemy**. The Pallava king can retreat behind Kanchi's walls, so the player must break his army before it reaches them.

Facts the game keeps honest:
- **No troop numbers and no speeches.** The exact site of Pullalur and the fighting are unknown; the battle is invented from the outcome, and the game says so.
- **Mahendravarman is painted with dignity**: a cultured king, artist and engineer, not a fool. The Pallava were great temple builders, so the Durg shows rock-cut and stone temples.
- **Era:** early seventh century CE, South India. Dhotis and sashes, light quilted or mail coats on soldiers, shields, spears, bows, war elephants with howdahs, horses. Not European, not Mughal.

This batch is **9 images**: 1 battlefield, 1 Durg, 3 Chalukya cards, 4 Pallava cards. Paint the battlefield and the Durg first.

## Same rules as before

- Cards portrait 5:7, as large as the tool allows. Durgs square, battlefield landscape 16:9. No text, borders or watermarks. Add "full figure with clear space above the head and below the feet" for single-person cards.
- **Chalukya cards**: the Chalukya era block from `PROMPTS-BATCH2.md`, as in batch 7.
- **Pallava cards and Durg**: the neutral frame, like Kanchi in batch 8. Use this clothing sentence in every Pallava prompt: *"South Indian early seventh century dress: a white or ochre dhoti with a sash, gold armlets and a tall conical crown for the king, soldiers in short quilted coats or light mail, round shields, spears and bows, a lion or bull (Nandi) banner."*
- **Battlefield**: the landscape style block from `PROMPTS-BATCH2.md` (top-down painted cloth map, details only around the edges, the middle open and calm).
- Upload cards and the Durg to `art/source/cards/`, the battlefield to `art/source/battlefields/`, named exactly as below.
