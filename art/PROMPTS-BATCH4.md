# YUDDH art prompts: batch 4, "The Indus Line"

The second battle of the Maurya campaign. Around 305 BCE, Seleucus Nicator, Alexander's general who inherited his eastern empire, crossed toward the Indus to win back the provinces Alexander had taken. Chandragupta met him. The war ended in a treaty: the Seleucids gave up the lands west of the Indus, and Chandragupta gave Seleucus 500 war elephants. The Greek ambassador Megasthenes later lived at Pataliputra and wrote about India.

Little is known about the actual fighting, so the cards below are drawn from the armies of the time, not from recorded battles.

This batch is 2 Durgs, 1 battlefield and 10 cards. Paint the two Durgs and the battlefield first, then the Seleucid cards, then the Maurya cards.

## Same rules as before

- For Maurya cards, paste the **Maurya and neutral era style block** first (from `PROMPTS-BATCH2.md`).
- For Seleucid cards, paste the same block, then add this line: **"The soldiers are Hellenistic Greek and Macedonian, painted in the same Indian miniature style: bronze helmets, linen armour, round shields, long pikes."**
- Cards are portrait 5:7. Durgs are square. The battlefield is wide (16:9 or wider).
- No text, borders or watermarks.
- Upload cards and Durgs to `art/source/cards/`, and the battlefield to `art/source/battlefields/`, named exactly as below.

## The setting (3)

**`durg_seleucid_camp.png` (Durg, square): the Seleucid camp**
```
A fortified Hellenistic army camp on a dry plain west of a great river: a ditch and earth rampart topped with sharpened stakes, square canvas tents behind, a tall pole with a bronze Macedonian star shield, sentries in crested bronze helmets, the evening light low and red. Strong, foreign, orderly.
```

**`durg_sindhu_kotta.png` (Durg, square): the Maurya fort on the Indus**
```
A Maurya frontier fort on the high east bank of the Indus river: thick mud-brick walls with wooden galleries and watchtowers, a gate with a saffron Maurya banner, war elephants standing in the shade below the walls, the wide river glittering beyond. Solid and watchful.
```

**`indus.png` (battlefield, wide): the crossing of the Indus**
```
A vast panoramic view of the Indus river valley at dawn from a high bank: the wide braided river with sandbanks and shallow fords, a Hellenistic army with long pikes massing on the far western bank, a Maurya army with elephants and chariots drawn up on the near eastern bank, mountains of the northwest on the horizon. Painted as a wide battle map, with clear open ground in the middle for the battle.
```

## The Seleucid army (6)

**`se_seleucus.png`: Seleucus Nicator**
```
Seleucus Nicator, Alexander's general and now a king, a powerful older soldier with a short grey beard, wearing a bronze cuirass and a royal diadem, a purple cloak, holding a long spear, a war elephant's head painted in the background, his phalanx behind him. Proud, experienced, ambitious.
```

**`se_phalanx.png`: the pike phalanx**
```
A Macedonian pike phalanx advancing toward the viewer: rows of soldiers in bronze helmets holding very long pikes levelled forward, a wall of pike points, round shields slung on their shoulders, dust rising. Disciplined and terrifying, a moving hedge of iron.
```

**`se_hetairoi.png`: Companion cavalry**
```
Heavy Hellenistic cavalry charging, the nearest rider large in the foreground on a rearing horse, wearing a bronze helmet with a white crest and a linen cuirass, levelling a long lance, a purple cloak streaming, more riders behind in a wedge formation.
```

**`se_toxotai.png`: Cretan archers**
```
A group of Cretan mercenary archers on a rocky slope, the nearest drawing a powerful recurved bow, simple tunics and small shields on their backs, quivers on their hips, the others loosing arrows into the sky. Lean, tough professionals.
```

**`se_lithobolos.png`: the stone-thrower (an order card)**
```
A Hellenistic torsion catapult (a large wooden stone-thrower with twisted rope springs) being loosed by its crew, a round stone ball flying toward a distant fort, engineers in tunics shouting and winding the next shot. Clever engineering in the dust of a siege.
```

**`se_megasthenes.png`: Megasthenes the envoy (an order card)**
```
A Greek ambassador in a simple white chiton and himation, middle-aged, holding a scroll and a stylus, standing in a Maurya palace courtyard with carved wooden pillars, looking around with curiosity at Indian courtiers and an elephant passing by. Observant, scholarly, peaceful.
```

## The Maurya army (4)

**`m_sindhu_nauka.png`: the river boats (an order card)**
```
A fleet of long wooden river boats rowed across the wide Indus at dawn, packed with Maurya archers and spearmen, oars flashing, a saffron banner on the leading boat, the far bank with enemy tents visible in the mist.
```

**`m_takshashila.png`: the Takshashila scholars (a unit)**
```
Scholars and teachers of Takshashila (Taxila) in plain cloth robes, gathered around an old teacher with a palm-leaf manuscript in a courtyard of stone and brick, one younger scholar pointing at a map of the river drawn in the sand. Wisdom serving the war: advisers, engineers, physicians.
```

**`m_gaja_sandhi.png`: the treaty of five hundred elephants (an order card)**
```
Chandragupta and Seleucus clasping hands in front of their two armies on a riverbank, between them a long line of decorated war elephants being led across toward the Greek side, scribes sealing a treaty on a low table. A moment of peace between two great empires.
```

**`m_ashva_dhanurdhara.png`: mounted archers**
```
Indian mounted archers galloping along a riverbank, the nearest rider large in the foreground turning in the saddle to shoot a long bow backwards, a quiver of arrows on his back, a cotton tunic and a turban, more riders behind kicking up spray from the shallows.
```
