# YUDDH art prompts: Phase 1 (first 20 cards + battlefield)

These prompts cover the first playable battle, **Maurya against the Nanda army at Pataliputra**. Every card in that battle is listed here.

## How to use this sheet

1. **Paste the style block first, then the card's prompt, every time.** The shared style block is what keeps 200 images looking like one set.
2. **Size:** portrait 5:7. Generate at the largest size your tool allows (aim for 1500 × 2100 px).
3. **Composition:** keep the subject inside the middle 60% of the frame. The top is cut into an arch and the bottom sits behind the name ribbon.
4. **No text or borders in the picture.** The game draws the frame, the name and the numbers.
5. **Save each file under the name shown**, for example `n_padati.png`. Send them to me in the chat and I convert them to game files.
6. Make two or three versions of each card and send only your favourite.
7. Check that your image tool's terms allow commercial use before the Play Store launch.

## Style block (paste before every card prompt)

```
Indian miniature painting in the style of the Mughal-Rajput court ateliers, reimagined as rich game card art. Mineral pigments: lapis blue, vermilion, malachite green, yellow ochre, touches of real gold leaf. Fine brushwork, flat decorative perspective with gentle depth and soft modelling of faces and muscles. Warm oil-lamp light from the upper left. Clothing, weapons and architecture of ancient India, around 320 BCE, as seen in the stone reliefs of Sanchi and Bharhut: cotton dhoti and uttariya, cloth turbans, heavy gold and bead jewellery, no stirrups, no armour plates, no metal helmets. Single clear subject, centred, full of character, readable at small size. Dark warm background that fades toward the edges. No text, no letters, no frame, no border, no watermark.
```

**Things to avoid** (paste into a "negative prompt" box if your tool has one, otherwise add "avoid:" and the list):

```
text, letters, signature, frame, border, modern clothing, medieval European armour, plate armour, metal helmets, stirrups, guns, Mughal-era turbans with plumes, Bollywood costume, 3D render, photograph, anime, cartoon, blurry, extra fingers, cropped head
```

## Battlefield (1 image)

**Size:** landscape 16:9, as large as your tool allows (aim for 3840 × 2160). Save as `pataliputra.png`.

```
Top-down painted cloth map of a battlefield on the Ganges plain beside the timber walls of ancient Pataliputra, in the style of an Indian miniature painting on aged cotton cloth. The river curves along the top edge, fields and mango groves along the sides, the city's wooden palisade and gate towers at the bottom edge. The wide middle band of the image is open dusty ground with very little detail, slightly darker, so playing cards can sit on it. Mineral colours: ochre, indigo, malachite green, faded vermilion, gold-leaf touches. Soft folds and texture of old cloth. No text, no labels, no compass, no people in the middle band.
```
